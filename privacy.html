James Family Wall - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

James Family Wall is a privately hosted household dashboard powered by Prism. It is intended for personal family use and is not open for public registration.

Information accessed

With the Google account holder's permission, the dashboard accesses Google Calendar information, including calendar names, event titles, dates, times, descriptions and other event details.

How information is used

Calendar information is used to display household schedules and, when enabled, create, update or delete calendar events at the account holder's request.

Storage and sharing

The dashboard is self-hosted on household-controlled devices. Calendar information and connection credentials may be stored locally by the dashboard to support its features. The household does not sell calendar data or intentionally share it with advertisers.

Google Calendar data is exchanged with Google through its authorised API. The underlying Prism software may have its own technical data-handling behaviour.

Access and deletion

The Google account holder can revoke access at any time through their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Locally stored dashboard data can be removed by the household administrator.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, use the support email shown on the James Family Wall Google authorisation screen.
